    05 — where they came from
    
The family tree

    

      Uno is Crazy Eights wearing a hat. Ludo is Pachisi with the interesting
      rule removed. House rules cluster by descent, which is why the
      same arguments keep reappearing in different games.
    

  

  







  

    06 — not everyone plays it your way
    
The same game,
played differently

    

      Some of these rules are standard in one country and unheard of in the next.
      If you have ever played with someone from elsewhere and both been certain,
      this is usually why.
