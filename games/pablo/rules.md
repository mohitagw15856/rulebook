# Pablo

Also played as **Cabo** and **Cambio**. Four cards in front of you, face down,
and you are allowed to look at two of them — once, at the start. Everything
after that is memory.

## Setup

Deal four cards face down to each player, arranged in a two-by-two square. Do
not look at them.

Each player may then look at **exactly two** of their own four cards, once. Then
they go back face down and stay that way.

Place the rest of the deck face down as the stock and turn one card to start the
discard pile.

## Playing a turn

Draw one card, either from the stock or the top of the discard pile. Then choose:

- **Swap** it for one of your four face-down cards, putting the old one on the
  discard pile. You may look at the card you are putting down, but not at the
  one you are picking up if it came from the stock.
- **Discard** it directly, using its power if it has one.

## Card powers

There is no publisher and therefore no official set. The most common is:

| Card | Power |
| --- | --- |
| 7 or 8 | Look at one of your own cards |
| 9 or 10 | Look at one card belonging to another player |
| Jack or Queen | Swap any two cards on the table, without looking |
| Black King | Worth zero points |

**Agree the set before dealing.** Every group plays this differently and every
group assumes theirs is standard.

## Calling Pablo

Instead of drawing, you may call "Pablo" if you believe you have the lowest
total. Every other player takes one final turn, then all cards are revealed.

- **You are lowest** — you score zero for the round.
- **You are not** — you take a penalty, most commonly 10 points plus your hand.

## Scoring

Number cards count face value, Jacks and Queens count 10, Kings count 10 except
the black Kings which count zero, and Aces count 1.

Play rounds until somebody passes 100 points. **Lowest** cumulative score wins —
this is a game you are trying to lose.
